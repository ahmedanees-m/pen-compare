PEN-COMPARE Documentation
==========================

**PEN-COMPARE** is the capstone of the PEN-STACK framework - a hierarchical certification
system that classifies genome editors as TRUE_WRITER, PROBABLE_WRITER, EMERGING_WRITER,
or NOT_WRITER using five pre-registered gates (v3.2).
